Preparation checklist

  • Translate ambiguous business needs into defensible reference architectures.
  • Map cost, latency and reliability requirements to production choices.
  • Design safety controls that fail closed.
  • Use evaluations as acceptance criteria and release gates.
  • Route decisions by confidence, reversibility and business impact.
  • Communicate trade-offs and create durable operational handoffs.

Architect – Foundations and Architect – Professional are separate certifications; Foundations is not a formal prerequisite for Professional.
