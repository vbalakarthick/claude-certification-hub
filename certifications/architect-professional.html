Recommended study order

💡 HUB RECOMMENDATION
  1. Read the current Professional exam guide and identify which areas require architecture judgment rather than implementation detail.
  2. Complete Claude Platform & Solution Design first and connect every pattern to a real enterprise use case.
  3. Study production integration together with cost, latency, reliability and operational constraints.
  4. Treat safety and governance as architecture: authorization, fail-closed controls, evidence and auditability.
  5. Practice stakeholder scenarios involving trade-offs, lifecycle ownership, acceptance criteria and operational handoff.
  6. Use optional practice courses to sharpen scenario judgment, not to memorize answers.
