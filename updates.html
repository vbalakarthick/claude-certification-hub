Certification change log

What changed?

A dated record of program changes that are easy to miss in old blog posts and study guides.

01 Sep 2026

Temporary Global Premier 100% exam discount has ended

The FAQ stated that the special Global Premier 100% discount ran through Aug 31, 2026. Standard Select, Preferred and Global Premier partner discounts now apply.

30 Jun 2026

Pearson exam delivery + Credly badging

Certification delivery moved to Pearson and existing digital credentials moved to Credly.

30 Jun 2026

Architect – Foundations price changed

Registrations from June 30 onward use the current $125 list price rather than the earlier $99 price.

2026

Credential validity standardized at 12 months

The current FAQ states certifications are valid for 12 months and older 6-month credentials were extended automatically.

How to read this log

Entries summarize only changes we can tie to an official source. When details are uncertain, we leave them out rather than present speculation as fact.
