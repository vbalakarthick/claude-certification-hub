Current program snapshot

Important logistics at a glance.

Partner-only access

Certification is currently available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations using a recognized partner-company email.

Pearson delivery

Exams are delivered online with proctoring or at Pearson test centers.

Retake rules

Waiting periods increase after failed attempts: 14 days, then 30 days, then 90 days; up to 4 attempts per rolling 12 months per exam.

Recertification

Credentials are valid for 12 months. Anthropic says on-time renewal will use a free, non-proctored update assessment.
