7-day sprint

For experienced practitioners or final revision.

  1. Scope: Read the current exam guide and map every task statement.
  2. Core: Review Claude platform/model fundamentals and role-specific concepts.
  3. Build: Prompting, tools, context and workflow/agent patterns.
  4. Production: Evals, security, cost, reliability and governance.
  5. Scenarios: Practice trade-off decisions; explain why each alternative is weaker.
  6. Repair: Re-study your weakest domains only.
  7. Exam readiness: Light review, Pearson system/ID checks, rest.
14-day foundation

For deeper learning and retention.

  1. Exam guide + diagnostic self-assessment.
  2. Claude/product/model fundamentals.
  3. Prompt engineering.
  4. Context engineering and knowledge strategy.
  5. Tool use.
  6. MCP / integrations.
  7. RAG and grounding.
  8. Agents and orchestration.
  9. Claude Code / developer workflows where relevant.
  10. Evaluation and observability.
  11. Security, governance and responsible AI.
  12. Architecture / business trade-offs for your role.
  13. Original practice + weak-area review.
  14. Light revision + exam logistics.
