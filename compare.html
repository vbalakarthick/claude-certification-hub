Which Claude certification should you take?

Choose based on the work you already do — not simply on which badge sounds more advanced.

CertificationPrimary audienceLevelPriceCPN tierMain emphasis
Associate – FoundationsConsultants, sellers, delivery leads, professional usersFoundations$99NoPractical Claude use, evaluation, workflows, governance
Developer – FoundationsHands-on engineersFoundations$125YesAPI, tools, agents, Claude Code, MCP, evals, security
Architect – FoundationsSolution / AI architectsFoundations$125YesArchitecture, tools/MCP, prompts, context, reliability
Architect – ProfessionalExperienced enterprise AI architectsProfessional$175YesEnterprise design, integration, safety, lifecycle, stakeholders

If you guide business adoption…

Start with Associate – Foundations.

If you build the system…

Developer – Foundations is the natural fit.

If you design the system…

Architect – Foundations maps to that role.

If you govern enterprise delivery end to end…

Architect – Professional is designed for that scope. There is currently no formal prerequisite requiring Architect Foundations first.

Anthropic describes Architect Foundations and Professional as separate certifications. Foundations does not automatically upgrade to Professional.
