Exam logistics & policies.

The operational details that can derail preparation if you leave them until exam day.

Eligibility & access

  • Certification is currently available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations.
  • Registration requires a recognized partner-company email domain; personal email addresses do not work.
  • You must be at least 18 years old.

Delivery

Current exams are delivered through Pearson, either online proctored or at a Pearson test center. Allow extra seat/check-in time beyond the 120-minute exam window where applicable.

Results & scoring

The exams use a scaled score with 720 as the passing score. Pearson score reports are available from the Pearson account dashboard after the exam according to the current FAQ.

Retakes

  • After first failed attempt: 14-day wait.
  • After second failed attempt: 30-day wait.
  • After third failed attempt: 90-day wait.
  • Maximum 4 attempts per rolling 12 months, per exam.
  • Retakes use the full exam fee, with applicable partner discount.

Certification validity & renewal

Certification is valid for 12 months from the earned date. Anthropic states that on-time recertification will involve reviewing changes and completing a free, non-proctored Partner Academy assessment. If a credential lapses, the full exam is required again.

Practice exams

The prior practice exam was retired during the move to Pearson. Current exam guides include sample questions that demonstrate style and format.

Accommodations

Requests go through Pearson. Anthropic recommends requesting accommodations at least 10 days before the planned exam date.

Exam-day checklist

  • Confirm your legal name matches your ID and Pearson registration.
  • Run the online system test well ahead of time if testing remotely.
  • Use a clean, compliant testing environment and reliable connection.
  • Do not rely on browser translation tools; exam and prep content are currently English-only.
  • Re-read the current exam policy directly before the appointment.
