How registration works

Anthropic first. Pearson second.

  1. Sign in to Anthropic Partner Academy using your recognized partner-company email.
  2. Open the certification page above and register/pay for the exam.
  3. Follow the Pearson link provided after registration / by email.
  4. Choose online proctored or a Pearson test center and select your appointment.
  5. Check your legal name against your government-issued ID before exam day.

Registration validity

The current Anthropic FAQ says an exam registration is valid for 5 years after registration.

Appointment changes

Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before the appointment to avoid forfeiting the exam fee.

Exam format

All four exams currently allow 120 minutes, with roughly 135 minutes total seat time including check-in and survey.

Closed book

Notes, documentation, AI assistants and browser translation tools are not permitted during the proctored exam.
